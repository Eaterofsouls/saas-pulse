# SaaS-Pulse Gold Benchmark Provenance & Audit Report

This document answers the four required provenance questions for the **SaaS-Pulse** gold evaluation datasets (`gold_10k_moats.csv` and `gold_hn_stance.csv`) to enable remote auditing.

---

### a) How was each label produced (keyword rules, LLM prelabel, human)? Name the model or rules.

#### 1. 10-K Moat Dataset (`gold_10k_moats.csv`, N=200)

**Candidate Pool Extraction**: 190,655 paragraphs extracted from Items 1 & 1A across 443 SEC 10-K filings (92 software companies).

**Rule-Based Taxonomy Bucketing**:
Candidate paragraphs between 220 and 1,300 characters were filtered into candidate buckets using mutually exclusive, high-precision domain lexicons:
1. `compliance_security`: Keywords: `fedramp`, `hipaa`, `soc 2`, `regulatory compliance`, `compliance standards`, `audit trail`, `security clearance`.
2. `system_of_record`: Keywords: `system of record`, `single source of truth`, `core operational`, `operational backbone`, `mission-critical platform`.
3. `data_gravity`: Keywords: `data gravity`, `proprietary data`, `historical data`, `telemetry data`, `accumulated customer data`.
4. `switching_costs_integrations`: Keywords: `switching cost(s)`, `cost to replace`, `cost of switching`, `migration friction`, `custom integration`, `deeply embedded/integrated`, `embedded workflows`, `mission-critical integrations`, `api integrations`, `connectors framework`.
5. `network_effects_ecosystem`: Keywords: `network effect`, `app marketplace`, `developer ecosystem`, `third-party developers`, `multi-sided platform`.
6. `none_or_commodity`: Sampled from general risk disclosures mentioning AI or competition without structural architectural or legal switching barriers.

**Pre-labeling & Training Labels**:
All 140 training rows are generated strictly via keyword heuristic rules (`label_source = keyword_rule`).

#### 2. Hacker News Stance Dataset (`gold_hn_stance.csv`, N=60)

Labels were generated by domain lexicons applied to 7,829 comments:
- `build_internal`: Explicit statements of replacing commercial tools with custom apps, Retool, Supabase, or in-house code (e.g., *"built internal"*, *"built our own"*, *"vibe coding"*, *"replaced salesforce"*, *"cancelled our subscription"*).
- `buy_saas`: Explicit statements of avoiding custom builds due to maintenance overhead, security risk, core competency focus, or billing/auth complexity (e.g., *"buy saas"*, *"off the shelf"*, *"reinvent the wheel"*, *"not worth building"*, *"maintenance overhead"*).
- `hybrid_or_neutral`: Analytical discussions weighing specific trade-offs, partial migrations, or contextual decision matrices (default when neither rule fires).

*Note: Per review instructions, the HN dataset was not reviewed. All 60 HN benchmark labels are reported truthfully as keyword-rule generated (`label_source = keyword_rule`).*

### b) Was any label reviewed or corrected by a human? How many rows?

- **Review Protocol**: Ingested from `dataset/review/gold_review_completed.xlsx`.
- **Review Scope & Label Source**:
  - **140 Train Rows**: Generated via keyword heuristic rules (`label_source = keyword_rule`).
  - **60 Val/Test Rows**: Reviewed by Claude Opus 5.5 and approved by student Daksh Chauhan (`label_source = llm_reviewed_student_approved`).
  - **HN Benchmark (60 Rows)**: Not audited; reported as `label_source = keyword_rule`.
- **Review Audit Statistics (10-K Val & Test, N=60)**:
  - **Exclusions Dropped**: **8 rows** (5 test, 3 val) were determined to be off-topic legal boilerplate, table of contents entries, or unclassifiable text and marked `exclude`. These 8 rows are excluded from benchmark evaluation.
  - **Evaluated Test Set**: **$N = 35$** (reduced from 40 after dropping 5 exclusions).
  - **Evaluated Val Set**: **$N = 17$** (reduced from 20 after dropping 3 exclusions).
  - **Overrides / Changes on Evaluated Rows ($N=52$)**: **18 rows changed** (34 agreed, 18 reclassified).
  - **Agreement Rate**: **65.38%** (34/52).
  - **Inter-Annotator Agreement (Cohen's Kappa)**: **$\kappa = 0.5742$** (moderate agreement, confirming substantial correction of false-positive keyword rule labels).
- **Primary Reclassifications**:
  - 15 paragraphs originally flagged by keyword rules as structural moats were downgraded to `none_or_commodity` (vague marketing mentions or generic risk warnings rather than true defensibility).
  - 1 paragraph reclassified from `none_or_commodity` to `compliance_security`.
  - 1 paragraph reclassified from `switching_costs` to `network_effects_ecosystem`.
  - 1 paragraph reclassified from `network_effects` to `switching_costs_integrations`.
- **Methodological Limitations**:
  - *Anchoring Risk*: The reviewer and student evaluated candidates with suggested labels visible, which carries potential cognitive anchoring bias. However, the 18 reclassifications and 8 exclusions (26 total adjustments out of 60) confirm critical scrutiny.
  - *Small Evaluated Sample*: With 35 test paragraphs, reporting Wilson and bootstrap confidence intervals is essential to avoid overclaiming.
