## 3. Methodological Notice: Anchoring Risk

Because the reviewer evaluated paragraphs with `suggested_label` visible in the adjacent column, **anchoring bias is possible**. 
Specifically:
- High agreement (100%) reflects confirmation of the high-precision keyword definitions on this filtered subset.
- However, cognitive anchoring to the pre-populated suggestion cannot be ruled out.
- This is explicitly acknowledged and highlighted on Slide 12 (Threats to Validity & Limitations) and in `GOLD_PROVENANCE.md`.
